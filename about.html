1975

The Foundation

PHATRADE was founded in Egypt in 1975, trading spices and herbs sourced from the fertile Nile Delta and Upper Egypt. As demand from international fragrance and flavour houses grew, the company invested in extraction infrastructure and cultivated its own aromatic crop farm in Al-Beheira Governorate near Sadat City.

1987

Manufacturing Begins

Production of essential oils began at the Sadat City facility in 1987, marking the transition from raw-material trader to fully integrated manufacturer. An in-house Gas Chromatography laboratory followed in 1989, giving PHATRADE the analytical capability to verify purity independently, a standard central to every batch produced today.

Today

Our Mission

PHATRADE delivers authentic, traceable, Egyptian-origin aromatic ingredients to B2B buyers worldwide. Backed by GFSI, HACCP, FDA, and Kosher certifications, and operating two integrated facilities, we supply fragrance, flavour, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical manufacturers across five continents.

Our Core Values

Purity

Every product is GC-verified against international reference standards. Zero synthetic additives, zero adulterants. Every batch, every time.

Full Traceability

Complete batch documentation links every shipment back to its source harvest lot, farm record, extraction run, and GC analysis report.

GFSI, HACCP, FDA, and Kosher certifications ensure our products meet the requirements of the world's most regulated markets in food, fragrance, and cosmetics.

Sustainability

Our own farm and local sourcing reduce the supply-chain distance between field and finished ingredient, supporting Egyptian agriculture and reducing environmental impact.
