Who We Are

Egypt's Leading Aromatic Ingredient Manufacturer

Founded in 1975, PHATRADE has grown from a spice and herb trading company into one of Egypt's most trusted producers of essential oils, concretes, and absolutes. Every ingredient we supply is cultivated on our own farm, extracted at our Sadat City facility, and GC-verified at our Obour City laboratory before it reaches you.
