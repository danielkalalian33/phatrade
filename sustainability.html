Origin & Sourcing

Local Sourcing

PHATRADE has cultivated and sourced its aromatic raw materials from Egyptian soil since its founding in 1975. Our own farm in Al-Beheira Governorate, near Sadat City, produces bitter orange flowers, geranium, basil, and violet.

  • Own farm in Al-Beheira Governorate
  • Direct control over cultivation & harvest
  • Supply chain kept entirely within Egypt
  • Full traceability from field to shipment

By eliminating intermediary steps and keeping the entire supply chain close to home, PHATRADE guarantees the traceability and freshness that B2B buyers in fragrance, flavour, and cosmetic industries demand. Sourcing locally is both a logistical advantage and a community commitment.

Our Process

Farm to Ingredient

Every PHATRADE essential oil travels a deliberate, controlled journey, from the soil of our Egyptian farm to a labelled, documented ingredient ready for your formulation.

01

Cultivation

Crops are grown on our own farm in Al-Beheira Governorate. Each species is cultivated to its optimal growing season, with harvest timing managed to capture peak aromatic content.

02

Extraction

Fresh plant material is processed immediately at our Sadat City facility by steam distillation or solvent extraction. With 4 lines, 15 extractors, 7 distillers, ensuring large harvests are handled promptly.

03

Quality Control

Every batch undergoes GC analysis at our Obour City laboratory. A retained sample is archived with the full batch record, providing end-to-end traceability from harvest to shipment.

04

Shipment

Approved batches are packaged and dispatched from Obour City, 15 minutes from Cairo Airport, with TDS, SDS, and Certificate of Analysis included for every consignment.

Growing Practices

Responsible Farming

On our farm in Al-Beheira Governorate, careful attention to each crop's natural growing cycle underpins both the aromatic quality of the finished ingredient and the long-term health of the land. We work with each plant's seasonal rhythm, not against it.

  • Seasonal planting schedules aligned to natural vigour
  • Harvest at peak aromatic concentration, not convenience
  • Water management suited to Egyptian growing conditions
  • Pruning and rotation to sustain productive lifespan
  • No unverified certification claims or synthetic inputs
