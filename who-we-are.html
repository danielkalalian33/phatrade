Our Story

From Spice Trader to Global Ingredient Supplier

Founded in 1975 in Cairo, PHATRADE began as a spice and herb trading company supplying the Egyptian market. Over five decades we have grown into one of Egypt's most respected producers and exporters of natural aromatic ingredients.

Today we operate two integrated facilities — a head office, manufacturing plant, and GC laboratory in Obour City, and a 100-acre extraction factory and cultivation farm in Sadat City. Every ingredient we produce travels a fully documented, traceable journey from seed to shipment.

Our clients are fragrance houses, flavour manufacturers, cosmetic producers, and pharmaceutical companies across Europe, the Middle East, North America, Asia-Pacific, and Africa who rely on PHATRADE for consistent quality, reliable supply, and full traceability.
