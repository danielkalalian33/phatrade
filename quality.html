Standards & Compliance

Quality Assurance

Every batch of PHATRADE essential oils is produced and verified against internationally recognised food-safety and quality standards, from cultivation through to shipment.

Our Certifications

Internationally Certified

PHATRADE holds recognised certifications that validate our food-safety management, traceability systems, and suitability for global markets.

FSSC 22000 Certified

FSSC 22000 is a GFSI-recognised food safety management certification scheme built on ISO 22000. PHATRADE's FSSC 22000 certification confirms our food safety management system is independently audited and benchmarked against the highest international standards accepted by global retailers and manufacturers.

ISO 22000 Certified

ISO 22000 is the internationally recognised standard for food safety management systems. PHATRADE's certification confirms our end-to-end food safety controls, from raw-material sourcing through extraction, processing, and packaging, meeting the requirements of global food, fragrance, and flavour industry buyers.

ISO 14001 Certified

ISO 14001 certifies PHATRADE's environmental management system, demonstrating our commitment to reducing environmental impact across all operations. Our certified system covers resource consumption, waste management, and environmental performance at both our Obour City and Sadat City facilities.

ISO 45001 Certified

ISO 45001 is the international standard for occupational health and safety management. PHATRADE's certification validates our systematic approach to workplace safety, risk identification, and incident prevention across all production, extraction, and laboratory operations.

HALAL Certified

PHATRADE's HALAL certification verifies that selected products are produced in full compliance with Islamic dietary law, confirming that ingredients, equipment, and production processes meet HALAL requirements, opening access to markets across the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and global Muslim consumer segments.

FDA Registered

PHATRADE is registered with the U.S. Food & Drug Administration, allowing our products to be imported into the United States in full compliance with FDA regulations for food, flavour, and cosmetic ingredients.

Kosher Certified

Selected PHATRADE products carry Kosher certification, verifying that ingredients, production processes, and equipment meet Jewish dietary law requirements, opening access to kosher-conscious markets in food, beverage, and personal care sectors.

SEDEX Registered

PHATRADE is a registered Sedex member and has completed a SMETA (Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit), giving buyers transparent access to our labour standards, health and safety practices, environmental performance, and business ethics record for responsible sourcing compliance.

EcoVadis Committed

PHATRADE holds the EcoVadis Committed sustainability rating, recognising our commitment to responsible business practices across environment, labour and human rights, ethics, and sustainable procurement. EcoVadis is the world's largest and most trusted provider of business sustainability ratings.

In-House Testing

GC Laboratory

Our on-site Gas Chromatography laboratory provides independent verification of purity and chemical composition for every product we produce. No outsourcing, no delays.

3 GC Analysis Units
100% Batches Tested
0 Outsourced Tests

GC Analysis covers:

  • Chemical purity & absence of adulterants
  • Key constituent compound percentages
  • Consistency against reference chromatograms
  • Residual solvents in concretes & absolutes

3 Dedicated GC Analysis Units

Three Gas Chromatography units run continuously, each dedicated to specific product families. Every batch is analysed against internal reference standards and international pharmacopoeia specifications before release.

Retained Sample Archive

A reference sample from every production batch is archived under controlled temperature and light conditions for a minimum retention period. This allows post-shipment verification and supports swift resolution of any quality dispute.

Batch-Level Documentation

Each batch ships with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) generated from in-house GC results. Buyers receive the full analytical report, GC chromatogram, and batch reference number for seamless supplier-qualification submissions.

International Standards Compliance

All analyses are conducted against recognised international standards including ISO, IFRA, IOFI, and relevant pharmacopoeia monographs, ensuring results are accepted by buyers in Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

Farm to Shipment

Batch Traceability

Every PHATRADE batch is traceable through four documented stages, from cultivation on our own farm to final export, ensuring full transparency for supplier-qualification audits.

  1. 1

    Farm

    Crops including bitter orange flowers, geranium, basil, and violet are cultivated on PHATRADE's own Sadat City farm. Each harvest lot is assigned a batch ID at the point of picking.

  2. 2

    Production

    Raw material is processed by steam distillation or solvent extraction. The batch ID carries forward to extraction records, linking each production run to its source harvest lot.

  3. 3

    QC Laboratory

    Each batch undergoes GC analysis across three in-house GC units. A retained sample is stored alongside the batch documentation for future reference and traceability audits.

  4. 4

    Shipment

    Approved batches are labelled, packed, and documented at our Obour City hub. Shipping documents reference the original batch ID, providing end-to-end traceability for every export.
